My Technical Interests

As a Computer Science Software Engineering student at the University of Hertfordshire, I am interested in learning how technology works and how software is developed. Below are two areas that I would like to explore during my studies.

1. Web Development

One of my main technical interests is web development. I enjoy learning how websites are created using HTML and CSS. I am interested in understanding how different parts of a website work together to create a useful and attractive webpage.

During my university studies, I want to improve my web development skills by creating simple websites and learning more about responsive design. I believe these skills will be useful for my future career in software engineering.

2. Software Development

My second technical interest is software development. I am interested in learning how computer programs are designed and developed to solve everyday problems. I want to understand programming concepts and how developers create applications that people can use.

As a first-year student, I am still developing my technical knowledge. I hope to improve my programming, logical thinking, and problem-solving skills through university assignments and practical projects. I also enjoy working with others and sharing ideas when solving problems.
